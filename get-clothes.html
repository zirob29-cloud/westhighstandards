How students can get clothes privately

We offer multiple private ways for students to access clothing:

  1. Drop-in: Students can visit the closet during designated open hours (ask your counselor or front office for current times).
  2. Teacher/Counselor referral: Staff may issue a referral so students can pick items discreetly.
  3. Anonymous request: Students may fill a short confidential request form — staff will arrange pickup.

Privacy

All visits are confidential. We maintain student dignity at every step. If a student needs specific sizes or special items, the staff will coordinate privately.
