How partners can help

We welcome partnerships with thrift stores, laundromats, dry cleaners, local businesses, and volunteers.

  • Donation drives and drop-off locations
  • Free or discounted laundering of donated items
  • Volunteer sorting and distribution
  • In-kind donations (coats, shoes, new underwear)
